Who We Are

Ruis Hair Products Co., Ltd

Ruis Hair Products Co., Ltd was founded in 2005, and our factory is located in Shandong Province, China. We are specialized in providing 100% cuticle-aligned Remy hair extensions to salons and distributors all over the world, and they are all satisfied with our quality.

We have mature and professional production lines, skilled hand weavers, strict quality inspection procedures and perfect service. Many clients create their own brand with us — we offer packaging service and customized colors.

We are willing to help you obtain greater achievement in your business, and wish to establish a long-term, stable and win-win cooperation with you.
